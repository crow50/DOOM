Which kind?

  • Site - a geographic place, usually with an address.
  • Zone - a building or open yard within a site.
  • Shelf - fixed storage: shelving, racking, a cabinet.
  • Bin - something you can carry: a bin, tote, or crate.

Any location can hold items directly, and the tiers do not have to nest in order - a shelf can sit straight in a zone, which is how flammables end up racked in an open yard. Only a site takes an address.
